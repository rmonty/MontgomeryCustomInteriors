About Me

I'd love to help you make your house a home!

I graduated from Texas Tech University in 2000 with my interior design degree. I received my interior design license in 2001 and worked for several architects and other interior designers. In 2011, I started my own interior design business, Montgomery Custom Interiors.

I have thoroughly enjoyed working with many different clients to create the perfect space for their needs. If you're interested in learning more about my services, call or e-mail today to book a consultation!
