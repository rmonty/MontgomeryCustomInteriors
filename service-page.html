Service page

A more detailed look at the process behind a project.

This page stands in for the deeper Wix service pages and gives visitors a more complete explanation of how the work is approached.

What you get

  • Room review and direction setting.
  • Layout and furniture recommendations.
  • Finish, color, and accessory selection support.
  • Ordering and install coordination as needed.

Best for

  • Rooms that need a clearer visual plan.
  • Projects where choices have stalled.
  • Spaces that need to feel warmer and more finished.
  • Clients who want practical guidance without a lot of friction.
Step 1

Consult

Start with the room, the constraints, and the goals so the design work stays focused.

Step 2

Design

Refine the options into a clear direction that matches the room and the way it is used.

Step 3

Implement

Keep the selections, delivery, and install work aligned until the space lands as intended.
